# Session 2026-10-12 — v1 design

## Topic
First run. Designed v1 of an Android Pomodoro app (Python + Kivy) from the blurb:
adjustable intervals and repetitions; easy start/pause/reset; customizable alarm with
vibrate-only; persistent notification with countdown and pause/unpause.

## Confirmed restatement
- **Outcome:** Android Pomodoro timer. Work → short break ×N, then a long break, then
  stop with "Session complete". Auto-advance with an alarm at each change. Adjustable
  work/short/long lengths and N, applied from the next interval and remembered.
  One-tap Start/Pause/Reset (Reset = whole session). Alarm mode: bundled sound /
  vibrate-only / silent, following the phone's silent/DND. Persistent notification
  only while a session is active, with type, mm:ss, progress, and Pause/Resume. Survives
  swipe-away.
- **Users:** one person on their own phone; no accounts or sync.
- **Why now:** smallest complete timer; teaches background work, notifications, audio.
- **Success:** 1/1/1 min, N=2, lock the phone, swipe away → countdown in notification,
  alarms, pause/resume from notification, "Session complete".
- **Constraints:** Python + Kivy, Android first.
- **Out of scope:** stats/history, labels, custom sound files, skip interval, reboot
  recovery, iOS, custom sequences.

## Decisions
- Docs live in `docs/app/` (config in `.claude/grill-to-dev-tickets.json`).
- ADR-001: build APKs with the `kivy/buildozer` Docker image (Arch isn't a documented host).
- ADR-002: the service owns the session; UI and notification are remotes over OSC.
- ADR-003: remaining time is derived from an End time, never counted in ticks.
- ADR-004: desktop first (core + tests → Kivy UI → first APK → Android features).

## Assumptions (not asked; change them if they're wrong)
- Defaults 25 / 5 / 20 min, N=4. Ranges: work 1–90, short break 1–15, long break 15–60, N 1–12.
  (Revised 2026-10-13 by the learner; originally breaks 1–60. The break ranges meet only
  at 15, so a short break can equal but never exceed the long break, and each field is
  still validated on its own. Short = long = 15 is allowed on purpose.)
  Defaults revised the same day from 25 / 5 / 15: short 5 and long 20 are the low ends
  of the typical ranges on Wikipedia (short 5–10, long 20–30,
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomodoro_Technique#Description). Stance: a flexible
  tool with good defaults. The allowed ranges stay wide; only the defaults follow
  the typical values.
- The alarm plays once (a few seconds) and needs no dismissing.
- The main screen shows the interval type, mm:ss, and "Work k of N".
- The phone reboot ending the session also means no stale notification after a reboot.

## Open questions
- Foreground service type: `specialUse` fits a timer but needs a justification that
  Google Play reviews. Fine for sideloading; revisit before publishing. (US-009)
- Is Plyer's vibrator enough, or will it need PyJNIus Vibrator plus a ringer-mode check? (US-013)
- Can Kivy's SoundLoader play bundled sounds from the service process (no window)? (US-013 spike)
- Which clock? `time.monotonic` vs. a suspend-aware clock (`CLOCK_BOOTTIME`) on a phone
  that deep-sleeps. (US-002 / US-010)
- If the phone sleeps through several interval ends, should the alarm fire for
  each, or once? (US-003, a product question for the learner)
- What happens if N is lowered below the current work interval number mid-session? (US-005)
